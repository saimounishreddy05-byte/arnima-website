Refund & Return Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Return Eligibility

Because agricultural inputs (insecticides, fungicides, biological growth promoters) are sensitive formulations, returns are accepted strictly for unopened, factory-sealed products damaged during transport or containing manufacturing batch defects.

2. Transit Damage & Reporting Window

Damaged shipments or batch discrepancies must be reported within 7 days of delivery by contacting us at +91 99087 84646 or emailing arnima4646@gmail.com along with unboxing photos/video documentation.

3. Replacement & Refund Process

Upon verification of valid return claims, Arnima Agro will issue either a free replacement dispatch or a full refund back to the customer's original bank account within 5-7 business days.

4. Order Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled free of charge prior to warehouse dispatch. Once a transport shipment has been dispatched, cancellation is subject to transport return fees.
