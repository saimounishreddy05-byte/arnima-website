Cookie Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. What Are Cookies & Local Storage

Cookies and browser LocalStorage are small data files stored on your device to remember user preferences, such as selected product cart items and category display preferences.

2. Strictly Necessary Cookies Used

We use strictly functional browser LocalStorage (`arnima_cart`) to ensure your shopping cart selections persist across page refreshes. We do not use intrusive cross-site advertising cookies or sell user tracking profiles.

3. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can clear or disable LocalStorage and cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling essential storage may reset your active cart selections.
